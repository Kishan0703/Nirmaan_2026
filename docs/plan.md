# NIRMAAN 2026 — Event Plan

**Organizers:** Coding Club BMSIT × Alterino Club BMSIT
**Tagline:** Build. Innovate. Impact.
**Type:** National-level 24-hour innovation challenge for student builders across India.

## About
- National scale — open to student teams from colleges across India
- 24-hour sprint — idea to working prototype, one continuous day
- Real-world impact focus — solutions that matter beyond the stage

## Structure — Two Rounds
1. **Round 1** — Online PPT submission, open nationwide
2. **Shortlisting** — Top teams selected by evaluation
3. **Grand Finale** — Shortlisted teams, on-site 24-hour build-off in Bangalore
